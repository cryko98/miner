How it works

  1. 1

    Buy & hold $MINER

    Grab at least 100,000 $MINER on pump.fun. That's your mining licence — no tokens, no ranked play.

  2. 2

    Connect & play

    Connect Phantom, Solflare or Backpack and sign a message (no transaction, no fees). You get 5 ranked runs per 15-minute round; only your best counts.

  3. 3

    Round closes

    Every 15 minutes the round locks. The server has already verified every run by replaying it, so the top 10 is final the moment the clock hits zero.

  4. 4

    SOL hits your wallet

    The round's pool is split proportionally among the top 10 and sent straight from the reward wallet. You must still hold 100k $MINER at payout time.

Built to be fair

🎲

Server-made maps

Each ranked run gets a secret seed from the server. You can't pick a lucky map or know where the gold is before the hook swings.

🔁

Replay verification

Your browser never sends a score — only the ticks you fired on. The server replays them through the same engine and computes the score itself.

🤖

Bot filters

Runs finishing faster than real time, inputs faster than a human tap, or perfectly periodic firing are rejected before they touch the leaderboard.

👛

One wallet, one entry

100k $MINER per wallet, 5 runs per round, best score only. Splitting tokens across wallets earns nothing extra — playing well does.

FAQ

Where does the SOL come from?

pump.fun pays the token creator a share of trading fees. 70% of everything we receive goes into the reward wallet. Each round pays out a fixed percentage of that wallet, so rewards track trading volume — no fixed APY, no promises.

Do I need to pay to play?

No. There is no entry fee and nothing to buy in-game. You only need to hold 100k $MINER in the connected wallet.

Can't someone just hack the score?

No. The client only sends the list of ticks on which you fired. The server regenerates the map from its own seed, replays those inputs and computes the score. Impossible timings and replayed sessions are rejected.

Is this gambling?

No. You never stake or risk anything to play. Rewards are a community distribution of creator fees based on skill.
