FAQ

Where does the SOL come from?

pump.fun pays the token creator a share of trading fees. 70% of everything we receive goes into the reward pool. If volume is high, payouts are high; there is no fixed APY and no promises.

Do I need to pay to play?

No. There is no entry fee and no in-game purchase with real money. You only need to hold 100k $MINER in the connected wallet to be eligible for rewards.

Can't someone just hack the score?

No. The client only sends the list of when you fired the hook. The server regenerates the level from its own seed, replays those inputs through the same engine and computes the score itself. Impossible timings, duplicate replays and bot-like input patterns are rejected.

What stops someone from using 50 wallets?

Every wallet needs 100k $MINER, runs are capped per wallet per round, and the pool is split by score among the top 10 — splitting tokens across wallets doesn't give more share than playing well with one.

Is this gambling?

No. You never stake or risk anything to play. Rewards are a community distribution of creator fees based on skill.
